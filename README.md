One original one-line joke about office life: the office plant has survived three managers, two layoffs, and one merger — and it's the only thing in the building with job security.

> The office plant has survived three managers, two layoffs, and one merger — it's the only thing in this building with job security.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
